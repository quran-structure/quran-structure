# Research Methodology & Disciplinary Framework

**Project:** *The Qur'an in Conversation: A Study of Surah, Ayah and Thematic Connections*  
**Core Slogan:** *"Each surah is unique and has a different personality."*

---

## 1. Hermeneutical Foundations: Sunni Discipline

This research initiative operates strictly within the classical Sunni tradition of *Tafsir* and *ʿUlūm al-Qur'ān*. 

The Qur'an is treated in its canonical *muṣḥaf* arrangement (the ʿUthmānic codex). We study:
1. **Munāsabāt al-Suwar:** The relationships and thematic handoffs between consecutive surahs.
2. **Munāsabāt al-Āyāt:** Structural coherence, internal transitions, and thematic developments within a surah.
3. **Surah Personalities (Gharaḍ al-Sūrah & Jaww al-Sūrah):** Honoring the unique identity, spiritual atmosphere, cadence, and thematic axis that distinguishes each surah.
4. **Opening and Ending Correspondences (Maṭāliʿ wa Maqāṭiʿ):** Structural envelopes where a surah concludes by answering, completing, or contrasting with its beginning.

---

## 2. Evidence Hierarchy

Every claim, transition, and connection must be evaluated along this hierarchy:

1. **Qur'anic Arabic Text:** Direct textual evidence in canonical Hafs recitation.
2. **Authentic Hadith:** Validated prophetic traditions contextualizing surahs or pairings (e.g., *al-Zahrāwayn*).
3. **Classical Sunni Exegetes (Mufassirūn):**
   - Fakhr al-Dīn al-Rāzī (*Mafātīḥ al-ghayb*)
   - Burhān al-Dīn al-Biqāʿī (*Naẓm al-durar*)
   - Ibn al-Zubayr al-Gharnāṭī (*Al-Burhān fī tartīb suwar al-Qur'ān*)
   - Jalāl al-Dīn al-Suyūṭī (*Tanāsuq al-durar*, *Marāṣid al-maṭāliʿ*, *Al-Itqān*)
   - Badr al-Dīn al-Zarkashī (*Al-Burhān fī ʿulūm al-Qur'ān*)
   - Ibn ʿĀshūr (*Al-Taḥrīr wa al-tanwīr*)
   - Al-Ṭabarī, Al-Qurṭubī, Al-Baghawī, Ibn Kathīr.
4. **Scholarly Observation:** Clear textual features identified through systematic comparative study, explicitly flagged.

---

## 3. Strict Guardrails

- **No Forced Coherence:** We never force a simplistic slogan or identical theme across disparate surahs.
- **No Numerology or Hidden Codes:** Mathematical patterns or speculative numerology are strictly prohibited.
- **No Unattributed Claims:** Every quotation must have a traceable edition, volume, and page reference in the registered classical corpus.
