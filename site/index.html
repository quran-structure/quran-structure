Research Method & Disciplinary Discipline

1. Sunni Hermeneutical Grounding

All interpretive claims must derive from recognised Sunni authorities: Al-Ṭabarī, Al-Baghawī, Al-Qurṭubī, Ibn Kathīr, Fakhr al-Dīn al-Rāzī, Ibn ʿĀshūr, Al-Biqāʿī, Ibn al-Zubayr al-Gharnāṭī, Al-Zarkashī, and Al-Suyūṭī. Unattributed internet claims, sectarian polemics, and numerological speculations ("hidden codes") are strictly excluded.

2. Rigorous Confidence Hierarchy

Every connection is explicitly labeled with its evidentiary weight:

  • Explicitly stated by a classical mufassir: Directly documented with exact volume and page.
  • Supported by multiple tafsir sources: Concurred by multiple independent classical authorities.
  • Classical scholarly observation: Formulated by a scholar but subject to ongoing evaluation.
  • Textually observable: Clear textual handoff or shared phrasing without forced theological extrapolation.
  • Research hypothesis: Clearly flagged speculative connection awaiting peer review.

3. No Forced Coherence

We do not assume every surah pair shares a simplistic thematic slogan, nor do we impose artificial ring compositions or chiasms that distort the authentic text. Distinctive personalities are honored without homogenizing the Qur'an.
